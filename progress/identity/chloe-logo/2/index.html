01
Step 1

Pick 3 territories

Choose the most promising, strange or fertile ideas. They do not have to be our favourites.

Step 2

Collect real references

Find the actual source phenomenon, not logos inspired by it.

Step 3

Make quickly

Follow the rule. Abuse it. See what happens. Ugly work is welcome.

How to use this deck

One worksheet per territory.

Use the next three cards for three different territories. Fill them in digitally or print them. Each sheet asks the same useful questions, so the comparison later is fair.

02
Remember
  • Don't spend ages polishing.
  • Don't drift into generic tech shapes.
  • Don't judge too early.
  • Don't keep an idea alive out of politeness.
Bring back
  • source references
  • 3 verbs for each territory
  • 10–20 tiny experiments
  • one very small test
  • one larger / louder application
  • what worked, what failed, what surprised you
