Kids

Games, robots and things with wires.

Weekly coding classes and school holiday camps for children in Marrickville.

Make games, animate stories, build websites, experiment with electronics, program robots and explore what else technology can do. We provide the tools, the guidance and plenty of room for ideas of their own.

Weekly classes

A regular place to make things.

Our classes are project-led, not a fixed sequence of worksheets. Children might build a game one term, work with a robot another, or develop their own project over several weeks.

We assess each child's experience when they start and choose projects and tools that suit their skills and interests. Beginners get a guided place to start. Experienced makers don't have to start again every time somebody new arrives.

Which programs and languages do we use?

That depends on what they're making. Children might use Scratch to program with visual blocks, move into text-based languages such as Python, JavaScript or Lua, or use HTML and CSS to create websites. We also work with game-making tools, robotics, Raspberry Pi, microcontrollers, sensors and electronics.

Those are possibilities, not a checklist every child completes in one term. We choose technology because it's useful for a project, not because it's next on a syllabus.

What will they learn?

Children learn how code works by using it: sequences, events, loops, variables and logic, introduced when a project gives them a reason to understand them.

Just as importantly, they learn to plan an idea, build it, test it, find what went wrong and try again. As their confidence grows, they make more decisions and take more ownership. The goal isn't to follow instructions forever.

Small groups. Proper guidance.

We keep groups small, with approximately one tutor to seven children, so we can notice when somebody needs help — or when they're ready for something harder.

Children work in groups suited to their age and experience. Some sessions run two separate age groups at the same time. All equipment is provided, and families are welcome to see what their child has made at the end of a session.

Weekly classes at a glance

  • Ages: 7–17, in age-appropriate groups.
  • Sessions: 75 minutes each week during school terms.
  • Times: Mondays 4:45–6pm; Tuesdays 5:15–6:30pm; Saturdays 9–10:15am, 10:30–11:45am or 12:15–1:30pm.
  • Fees: $270 for a nine-week term. Children joining partway through pay an adjusted fee for the remainder of the term.
  • Where: The lab, Level 1, 230 Marrickville Road, Marrickville.

Most children continue from term to term, building on what they've already learned. We'll confirm the current term dates and available places when you enrol.
