Meet the lab / 03

People make interesting things with technology here.

We teach because we build. We build because we teach.

Who
Children, teenagers and adults. Beginners, experienced makers and people who aren't quite sure where to start.
What
Coding, AI, games, electronics, robotics, physical computing, websites, interactive projects and design.
Why
Making gives learning a reason. Understanding what you're doing gives you more say in what happens next.
How
Small groups, useful guidance, real projects and gradually more independence.
Where
Upstairs in Marrickville, Sydney.
When
Weekly classes, school holiday coding camps, adult workshops and special builds.

Qwerty Labs grew out of QwertyKids, founded in 2015. The studio is led by DC, whose work has taken her from early web experiments at ABC Science Online to digital projects for organisations including Microsoft Australia, then back to the question that started it all: what can you make with this?
