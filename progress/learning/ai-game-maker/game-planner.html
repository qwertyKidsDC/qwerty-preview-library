Tell us about your game.

There are eight questions and one optional twist. Write as much or as little as you need. The more specific you are, the more your first game will feel like yours.

01 · Title

02 · Game type

Pick the closest option. Your idea can be a mixture.

03 · Character

A person, creature, spaceship, sandwich… anything.

04 · Objective

Describe what you actually do in the game.

05 · Controls

Think about how someone will play on a computer.

06 · Challenges

Enemies, obstacles, a timer, tricky choices, physics…

07 · Scoring and winning

Decide what success looks like, and when a round ends.

08 · Look and feel

Choose a setting, colours or art style.
