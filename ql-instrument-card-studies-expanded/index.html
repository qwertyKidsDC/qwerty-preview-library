Grounding used this round: early internet art and the web as a medium; open design and in-process critique; printer marks / registration logic; Atkinson Hyperlegible; and hands-on interface metaphors.

Five genuinely different
territory options.

These are meant to feel different to a human at first glance, not just after reading the labels. Each option uses a different ancestral family, a different webpage structure, and a different visual grammar.

The most likely outcome for QL is still a hybrid later, but first these need to separate properly.
