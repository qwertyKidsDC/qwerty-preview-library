Make bigger things in our weekly classes and holiday camps.

AGES 13–17

More independence. More say in what you make.

Games, AI, electronics, robotics and bigger ideas. Start something new or bring a project of your own. We'll help you work out how to build it.

Weekly classes

Teens aged 13–17 make with us on Tuesdays and in all our Saturday sessions. Some are new to coding; others are working on projects that take weeks to get right.

Bring an idea, start something new or return to a project over the term. We help with the coding, design, electronics and tricky decisions along the way. Small groups mean there's room to work independently and someone to ask when you're stuck.

Building something ambitious? There's room for that here too. We can help you plan, prototype and debug it as part of your regular classes.

Tuesday: 5:15–6:30 pm.
Saturday: 9:00–10:15 am, 10:30–11:45 am and 12:15–1:30 pm.

Nine 75-minute sessions per term, $270. New students can join during a term if there's a suitable place. We'll help find a session that fits.

Some sessions are shared with children aged 7–12, but projects and guidance are tailored to each person's interests and experience. You don't have to make the same thing as everyone else.

We're upstairs at Level 1, 230 Marrickville Road. All equipment is provided.

Holiday camps

Teens aged 13–17 can join any of our general holiday camp days. Try something new, work on a more challenging project or spend the day developing an idea of your own.

Teen Cadets is a dedicated group for teenagers, so you'll be making alongside other teens.

General camp days are shared with children aged 7–12. Projects are tailored to each age and experience level, though you might be the only teen on a particular general camp day.

Our usual camp day runs 9:30 am–3:30 pm, with drop-off from 9:00 am and pickup by 4:00 pm. Teens can arrange a later start; ask when booking. Optional extended hours are available.

First day $145; additional days $130 each. Equipment is provided.
