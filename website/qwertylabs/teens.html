teensmake something bigger

Develop your own ideas in our weekly classes and holiday camps.

AGES 13–17

What would you make if you knew how?

Explore code, electronics, AI and design with people who can help you get an idea working. No experience needed.

Who?

For ages 13–17. You might be teaching yourself to code, looking for something more challenging than school offers or simply curious about how things work.

Some of our teens have been making with us since they were kids. Others arrive never having written a line of code.

You don't need a project in mind. Finding one is part of the fun.

What?

A game with its own mechanics, a website for something you care about, an interactive artwork or a device that collects data and sends it somewhere useful.

You might explore one technology in depth or combine several. We work with Python, JavaScript, Scratch, game engines, AI, Raspberry Pi, microcontrollers and sensors. We choose the tools to suit the idea and your experience.

At 14, Zach and Chloe built Project Code Cloud to help younger makers understand where their code goes. They combined programming, electronics and physical design into an interactive exhibit. It became a VIP judge's favourite at the global Coolest Projects 2026 showcase.

Your project doesn't need to be that ambitious. But if an idea grows into something you'd like to share with a wider audience, we'll help you explore what that could look like.

Why?

What have you been wanting to try?

Perhaps you've played a game and thought of a better way to do something. Seen a piece of technology and wondered how it works. Or had an idea that seemed a little beyond your reach.

Here's somewhere you can find out. Try an idea, see what happens and work out what you'd do differently.

You might discover an interest that influences what you study or do later. Or you might simply find something you love doing outside school. Both are good reasons to make things.

How?

We'll help you get started, whether you've got a project in mind or not.

As you become more capable, our role changes. Rather than showing you what to do next, we might discuss different approaches, introduce a technique you haven't encountered or help you work through a particularly stubborn problem.

With around one tutor for seven students, we have time for individual guidance and regular check-ins. Sometimes we explore an idea together; other times everyone is working on something different.

We've been teaching creative technology since 2016. Our tutors write code and make things too. It's a studio where you can talk through ideas, try things out and share what you've made. Read Zach's account of being a student here, written at 13.

If there's anything that would help you work comfortably in the studio, get in touch and we'll talk through which group might suit you.

“Being a student here for over three years has encouraged me to take the skills I learned in these classes into my everyday life.”

Zach · Student, writing at 13

Where?

Upstairs in Marrickville, Sydney's Inner West.

Level 1, 230 Marrickville Road, Marrickville NSW 2204.

Look for the entrance next to Pizza Hut, near Victoria Road. We've put together some tips for getting here.

Every student has their own computer. We provide all the equipment, so there's no need to bring a laptop.

“Code isn’t just typing y’know. It’s also building and modelling.”

Zach · Student, writing at 13

When?

Weekly classes

75 minutes weekly, usually nine sessions per term. Develop an idea over time, explore a technology in depth or return to a project across several terms. Join during a term when there's a suitable place.

School holiday camps

A day away from the usual routine to experiment, learn a new tool or get properly into a project. Come for one or several days, with different projects to explore.
