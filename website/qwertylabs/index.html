COOLEST PROJECTS

Zach and Chloe built a cloud that makes invisible code visible. An international Coolest Projects judge picked it as a favourite. We told the Prime Minister. His office wrote back to congratulate them on their creativity, innovation and technical skills. Not a bad result for a couple of makers from Marrickville.

MEET THE LAB

We've been making things with people in Marrickville since 2016. Around 1,500 young makers have come through our doors. We still squeal when the stuff we build does things.
