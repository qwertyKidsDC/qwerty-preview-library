Weekly classes Enrol in camps

AGES 7–17

A whole day of games, robots, electronics and making things with technology. Beginners get help getting started. Experienced makers can tackle bigger ideas.

Different projects on different days. Come for one day or several.

Weekly classes Enrol in camps

Who?

Children and teens aged 7–17, from complete beginners to experienced young makers.

Some children come with friends. Others arrive not knowing anyone. Many come back each school holidays.

We help beginners get started and give experienced children more challenging projects. Teens aged 13–17 are welcome on any general camp day, with age-appropriate challenges.

Depending on bookings, a teen may be the only teen camper that day. We think it's worth knowing this before booking.

What?

Games, robots, electronics, websites, virtual worlds and things that light up, move or respond to code.

Children might make a game with characters and levels, program a robot, build a movement detector with a micro:bit, experiment with VR or use AI to develop a game.

Beginners often make two or three smaller projects in a day. Children with experience may spend longer developing something more ambitious.

Different projects run on different days, so there's plenty to explore if you come back for more.

Why?

A whole day gives an idea room to grow.

There's time to learn something new, try it out, change your mind and see what happens.

Children learn coding, electronics, design and problem-solving as part of making things. Sometimes the interesting part is getting something working. Sometimes it's figuring out why it doesn't.

Camp is a chance to explore technology children might not have access to at home, with people who can help them take an idea further.

How?

Small groups, hands-on projects and individual guidance throughout the day.

Each day has a selection of projects suited to different ages and experience levels. Beginners start with guided activities; experienced children can modify projects, tackle harder challenges or explore something of their own.

We don't expect everyone to make the same thing at the same pace. Tutors work alongside children, explaining concepts, demonstrating techniques and helping them investigate problems. Groups are small, with around one tutor for seven children.

No coding experience is necessary. If your child has particular support needs or finds busy environments difficult, tell us when booking so we can talk through what might suit them.

Where?

Upstairs in Marrickville, Sydney.

Level 1, 230 Marrickville Road, next to Pizza Hut, near Victoria Road. Our studio is full of computers, robots, electronics, components and the sort of things that make children ask, “What's that?”

Every child has their own computer, and all project equipment is provided.

When?

A camp day

Camp runs from 9:30 am to 3:30 pm, with drop-off from 9:00 am and pickup by 4:00 pm. Optional extended hours are 8:00 am–5:30 pm.

Camp fees

First day $145. Additional days $130 each. Optional extended hours $55 per day.

Upcoming camps

Dates for the next school holiday programme will be added once confirmed. You can book one day or several, with different projects on different days.

Camp enrolment

Enrolment takes about two minutes, with no payment at the form stage. We'll confirm the details with you afterwards.
