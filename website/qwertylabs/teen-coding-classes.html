classestake an idea further

Weekly creative technology classes with time to develop your ideas and build your skills.

AGES 13–17

Some ideas need more than an afternoon.

Learn to code, explore new technology or develop a project of your own over several weeks or terms. We'll work alongside you.

Who?

For ages 13–17, from complete beginners to people who've been coding for years.

Perhaps you've started making a game at home, want to move into text-based programming or are looking for help with an idea you can't quite work out on your own.

We work in small groups and take age, interests and experience into account. Some sessions have separate groups running at the same time.

Some sessions are shared with children aged 7–12. Your work and the guidance you receive are tailored to you, not to the age of the youngest person in the room.

When?

Class times

Nine weekly sessions during school term. Each class is 75 minutes.

  • Tuesday: 5:15–6:30 pm
  • Saturday: 9:00–10:15 am
  • Saturday: 10:30–11:45 am
  • Saturday: 12:15–1:30 pm

Need a different time or a place on the waiting list? Tell us which class times suit you.

$270 for a nine-session term. New students can join partway through when there's a suitable place.

Where?

Upstairs in Marrickville, Sydney's Inner West.

Level 1, 230 Marrickville Road, Marrickville NSW 2204.

We're next to Pizza Hut, near Victoria Road. Our studio is full of computers, robots, electronics and projects in various stages of becoming something.

Every student has their own computer. We provide all the equipment, so there's no need to bring a laptop.

What?

Build a game and test how it plays, create a website for a real audience, or program a Raspberry Pi to collect sensor data and display it on a webpage.

A project might begin with a small experiment and grow as you discover what else it could do. You could spend several terms refining one idea or make a series of smaller things while exploring a particular interest.

We use Scratch, Python, JavaScript, HTML, game engines, AI, Raspberry Pi and microcontrollers. We'll help you choose the technology that makes sense for what you're building.

Curious about combining software, hardware and design? Take a look at Project Code Cloud, made by two of our teens.

Why?

Following a tutorial is one thing. Making something nobody's written a tutorial for is another.

A longer project brings questions you don't necessarily encounter in a tutorial. Is this the right approach? Why is it behaving that way? What could make it better?

Weekly sessions give you time to investigate those questions, develop your technical skills and become more confident making your own decisions.

You might eventually want to publish a game, share a project at a showcase such as Coolest Projects or explore an interest further.

How?

We work alongside you, in small groups, with more of a mentoring role as you gain experience.

New to coding? We'll start with guided projects that introduce useful concepts and techniques. When you're ready, we can help you work out what you want to make, break it into manageable pieces and get started.

Further along, we might review your code together, discuss a design decision or suggest ways to investigate a bug rather than fix it for you. There are no fixed levels.

With around one tutor for seven students, there's individual help when you need it and room to work independently. Your project doesn't have to be finished by the end of term.

It's an active studio, not a silent one. Tell us when enrolling if there's anything that would help you work comfortably here.
