BEFORE CLASS

Your weekly session is booked. Excellent. Here's what you need to know before arriving.

Your confirmation email contains your child's day, time and start date. This page covers the practical details that don't change each term.

YOUR FIRST CLASS

Classes run for 75 minutes. Check your booking confirmation for your child's day, time and first session.

No preparation is needed. Beginners get help getting started; experienced makers can pick up a project or try something new.

Please bring your child upstairs to the studio and let us know if their collection arrangements change.

WHAT TO BRING

No laptop or special equipment required. Every student has their own computer and we provide the technology for their projects.

A water bottle is useful. Please give us any relevant Medical Action Plans, medication information or support details before the first session.

Have an idea for something to make? Bring it along. It's fine to arrive without one too.

FIND THE LAB

Level 1, 230 Marrickville Road, Marrickville NSW 2204. We're upstairs, beside Pizza Hut near Victoria Road.

Time-limited street parking may be available nearby, and there's a council car park on Garners Avenue. Check the signs for restrictions.

Get directions to the Lab.

MAKING TOGETHER

Our studio is active and hands-on. We encourage questions, experiments and making decisions about what to build next.

We ask students to look after equipment, respect other people's work and follow safety guidance. Before the first class, please read the Booking Terms together.

If busy environments or particular learning challenges are tricky for your child, tell us what helps. We can plan for a suitable group.

NEED TO TELL US?

Running late, reporting an absence or changing collection arrangements? Please text us.

0405 950 850 is the easiest way to reach us while we're teaching.

See our Booking Terms for attendance changes and our Child Safety policy for how we look after children at the Lab.
