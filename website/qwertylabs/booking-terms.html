BOOKING TERMS

The practical details, so the interesting stuff can happen.

These terms apply to bookings for Qwerty Labs / QwertyKids ("Qwerty") classes, holiday camps, workshops and mentoring in Marrickville or online. Please read them with the relevant program description before booking. If a program has specific terms, we will explain them before you commit.

Booking and payment

Places are limited so we can give participants appropriate attention and supervision. A place is confirmed when Qwerty accepts the booking and the stated payment requirements are met. Fees, dates, session times, location, inclusions and any age or experience requirements are provided in the program information.

Weekly classes are generally enrolled by the term; full-term payment is preferred unless we agree otherwise. Please keep contact, emergency and collection information current. If booking details change, contact us as soon as possible.

Ages and groups

Our general weekly classes and holiday camps welcome ages 7–17, with activities and support adapted to each person's age, interests and experience. Some specialised programs may have narrower age or experience guidelines. Teens can join general camps even if no other teenager is booked that day; we are happy to discuss the likely group mix beforehand.

Respect and safety

Students are encouraged to explore, experiment, make mistakes and treat others kindly. Everyone must follow reasonable teacher directions, use equipment safely and respect other people's boundaries, privacy and projects. Bullying, discrimination, intimidation, harassment and deliberately unsafe behaviour are not acceptable.

We aim to make reasonable adjustments for disability and individual support needs. Please share relevant information before attendance so we can plan. If a serious difficulty arises, we will speak with the family, consider adjustments and respond proportionately. Immediate action may be necessary where safety is at risk. Any decision to end attendance and any associated fee outcome will be assessed fairly and consistently with applicable law.

Our Child Safety Policy applies to all children's programs.

Arrival and collection

Parents and carers are responsible for safe arrival and prompt collection. We release children only to authorised people, unless other arrangements have been agreed in advance. Independent departure requires prior parent or carer authorisation. Please tell us promptly about changes or delays.

Absences and make-up classes

If a student will miss a weekly class, please contact us before the session begins. We may offer a make-up class when a suitable group and place are available. Make-ups must be arranged in advance, are not guaranteed and usually cannot be replaced if missed.

Missed sessions for holidays, other activities or a change of plans do not automatically give rise to a refund or credit. This does not limit rights under Australian Consumer Law.

Illness

Please stay home if you have symptoms of a contagious illness or are not well enough to take part safely. We may contact a parent or carer to collect a child who becomes unwell.

For illness-related absences, we may offer a credit, subject to the circumstances and available places. We may ask for reasonable supporting information where necessary, without seeking more health information than we need. A discretionary credit is usually usable in the current or following term or camp. Legal entitlements are not affected.

CANCELLATIONS

Tell us in writing if you wish to cancel. Because we reserve limited places and arrange staffing in advance, a change-of-mind cancellation may involve reasonable costs or losses. We assess what is fair in the circumstances, taking account of notice given, costs incurred, steps we can take to reduce loss and whether the place is filled by another participant.

Our usual maximum change-of-mind deductions from fees paid are:

  • 21 days or more: Up to 20%
  • 14–20 days: Up to 50%
  • Fewer than 14 days: Up to 100%, but only where reasonable in the circumstances

These are ceilings, not automatic penalties. We will not retain more than is reasonable for the circumstances. If we can fill your place or avoid expenses, we take that into account. After a program begins, we assess any voluntary withdrawal in the same way, including what services have already been delivered. Special circumstances may also be considered.

These change-of-mind arrangements do not limit any right to a remedy under Australian Consumer Law, including where Qwerty does not supply a service with the required care and skill or as agreed.

Changes or cancellations by Qwerty

Sometimes we need to change a timetable, venue, teaching arrangement or program because of staff availability, insufficient enrolments, safety issues or events outside our control. We will notify affected customers as soon as reasonably practicable.

If Qwerty cancels a program before it begins, we provide a full refund of fees paid for that program. You may choose an alternative place or credit instead.

If sessions are cancelled or a program is interrupted, we will offer an appropriate reschedule, alternative service or refund for the part not provided, as the circumstances and consumer law require. We will not automatically replace an in-person program with online teaching and insist on full fees regardless of the change. Material changes will be discussed with affected customers.

Equipment, projects and personal belongings

Unless a program description says otherwise, we provide the equipment needed for studio activities. Please follow safe-use instructions and take reasonable care of equipment. Label personal items and avoid bringing valuables. We take reasonable care but are not responsible for every loss or accident; nothing here excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Students should not publish personal contact details, share passwords or use online platforms in ways that harm others. Appropriate permission is required before Qwerty publicly shares identifiable student images or work linked to a student's identity. See our Privacy Policy.

Emergencies

Please supply accurate emergency contacts and relevant health information. If urgent assistance is needed, we may call emergency services and take reasonable steps to protect the participant, while contacting the parent or carer as soon as practicable.

CONSUMER RIGHTS

Australian Consumer Law provides guarantees for services that cannot be excluded or restricted by these terms. If a service fails to meet a consumer guarantee, the remedy depends on the circumstances and may include having a problem fixed, cancellation or a refund. Our change-of-mind policy operates in addition to those rights.

Contact

For bookings, changes or concerns, call 0405 950 850 or use our contact form.
