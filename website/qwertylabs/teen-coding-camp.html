campsa day to experiment

School holiday camps for teenagers who want to try new technology or get stuck into a project.

AGES 13–17

You've got a day, a studio full of technology and people who know how to use it. What would you like to try?

Beginners welcome. Bring an idea or discover one here. Come for one day or several.

Who?

For ages 13–17, whether you're completely new to coding or already making things at home.

Come with a friend, come on your own, try a technology you've been curious about or spend some uninterrupted time making something. Many of our makers return during the holidays.

Our general camps are shared with children aged 7–12, but teens have their own work area and projects suited to their interests and experience. You might be the only teen camper on a particular day.

Where?

Upstairs in Marrickville, Sydney's Inner West.

Level 1, 230 Marrickville Road, Marrickville NSW 2204.

We're next to Pizza Hut, near Victoria Road. Our studio is full of computers, robots, electronics, components and projects in various stages of becoming something.

Every student has their own computer, and all project equipment is provided.

What?

A day is a good opportunity to get hands-on with something you haven't tried before. Experiment with AI, build a sensor-controlled gadget, make a game prototype or explore how a website works.

You might try two or three smaller projects to get to know a technology, or spend the day developing and testing one idea. If you've already got some experience, we'll help you find a challenge worth getting stuck into.

There's a different selection of projects on different days. You can also bring ideas for something you'd like to build and we'll help you work out what's possible with the time and equipment available.

Why?

Ever wanted to try something but didn't have the kit, the time or someone to show you how to get started?

Spend a day exploring a new interest without having to commit to a whole term. Or give yourself a stretch of time to make progress on an idea that needs more than a quick tutorial.

You'll use coding, electronics, design and problem-solving to get things working. You might leave with a prototype, a new skill or another idea worth exploring. A polished result isn't the only useful outcome.

It's a chance to explore technology you might not have access to at home and discover what you enjoy doing with it.

How?

The day starts with ideas. We'll help you choose from the available projects and technologies or talk through something of your own.

If you're just starting, we'll guide you through the unfamiliar bits. If you've got more experience, we can suggest ways to extend a project, test an approach or investigate a problem yourself.

You don't have to follow someone else's project at someone else's pace. With around one tutor for seven students, there's practical help throughout the day and room to make your own decisions.

No coding experience is necessary. If your teen has particular support needs or finds busy environments difficult, tell us when booking so we can talk through what might suit them.
