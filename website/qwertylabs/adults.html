At the Lab

Come in. Make something.

We're developing hands-on workshops for artists, designers, makers and other curious adults at our Marrickville studio. Experiment with real tools, ask questions and work alongside other creative people.

Explore electronics, creative coding, prototyping and interactive installations. The project comes first. We help you choose the tools to suit.

Interested in a studio workshop? Tell us what you'd like to explore. We're gathering interest as we plan our first sessions.

Mentoring

Bring your project. Let's work on it.

One-to-one project guidance, at the Lab or online. Plan a project, sketch an interface, test a prototype, untangle some code or work out why the thing isn't doing the thing.

Start from scratch or bring something you're already building. We'll work from your interests and experience.

Online workshops

Learn something new, wherever you are.

We're developing online workshops on creative coding, prototyping, AI and other ways of making things with technology. The format will depend on the topic.

One planned strand is Make It Well: exploring accessibility, safety, privacy and data awareness. Getting something to work isn't quite the same thing as making it good.

Dates aren't set yet. Tell us what interests you and we'll let you know when sessions are scheduled. No commitment required.

For groups

Learn something useful together.

We can plan focused sessions for creative teams and organisations, online or at the Lab. Bring a challenge or choose a topic to explore together.

From idea to prototype

Turn a rough idea into a clear brief, an interface or a working first version.

Make It Well

Explore accessibility, safety, privacy and data awareness in the context of your group's work. Practical guidance, not certification or legal sign-off.
