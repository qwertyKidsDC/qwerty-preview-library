adultswhat will you make?

Creative technology mentoring and workshops for curious adults, makers and creatives.

MAKE SOMETHING

An app, an interactive artwork or something that doesn't exist yet.

We'll help you work out how to make it. Beginners welcome, online or at the lab.

Mentoring

One-to-one help with a project of your own.

We'll help you choose the tools and show you how to use them. The project is yours.

From idea to prototype

Start from scratch, pick up an unfinished project or venture into unfamiliar territory. We'll help you work out the next step, including why the thing isn't doing the thing.

Register your interest in mentoring

Group workshops

Useful things to learn together.

You might be making an app and somebody else might be building an interactive artwork. There's quite a bit you can learn together.

Make It Well

Getting something to work isn't quite the same thing as making it good.

Explore accessibility, safety by design, responsible AI and the responsibilities that come with creating technology for others.

What happens when someone uses your creation in a way you didn't anticipate? What are you responsible for as its creator? And what does the law have to say about it?

Join us alongside your individual mentoring or simply come along to explore something new. You don't need a project of your own.
