adultswhat will you make?

Creative technology mentoring and workshops for curious adults, makers and creatives.

MAKE SOMETHING

An app, an interactive artwork or something that doesn't exist yet.

We help adults explore technology, develop new skills and bring ideas to life. Beginners welcome, online or at the Lab.

Mentoring

Your idea. Your project. One-to-one guidance.

Learn through making something that matters to you. We'll work with your interests, experience and ambitions, helping you choose the tools and develop the skills you need along the way.

From idea to prototype

Explore what's possible, shape your idea, experiment with technology and build something that works.

Start from scratch, pick up an unfinished project or venture into unfamiliar territory. We'll help you work out the next step, including why the thing isn't doing the thing.

Group workshops

Different projects. Shared discoveries.

Some things are particularly useful to learn together. Our group workshops explore ideas, tools and approaches that have applications across all sorts of creative technology projects.

Join us alongside your individual mentoring or simply come along to explore something new. You don't need a project of your own.

Make It Well

Getting something to work isn't quite the same thing as making it good.

Explore accessibility, safety by design, privacy and responsible AI. Consider the people who might use the things we make and the decisions we can make as creators.

Makers teach

Learn from someone who makes things.

Qwerty Labs brings together more than 20 years of professional digital production and over a decade of teaching creative technology.

Real projects. Real tools. No requirement to become a developer first.
