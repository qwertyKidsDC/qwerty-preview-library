BEFORE CAMP

A whole day to make things. Here's the practical stuff so everything runs smoothly.

Your confirmation email has the days you've booked and any individual arrangements. This page covers what stays the same.

CAMP HOURS

Standard camp: 9:30 am–3:30 pm. Drop-off is from 9:00 am and pick-up is by 4:00 pm.

Optional extended hours: 8:00 am–5:30 pm by arrangement. Please text by 9:00 pm the night before if you need early drop-off. Let us know as soon as possible about a late pick-up.

Teens can arrange a later start. If your booking confirmation specifies different hours, follow those agreed arrangements.

WHAT TO BRING

  • Packed lunch and recess
  • Extra snack if staying for extended hours
  • Hat and raincoat for lunchtime outings
  • Medical Action Plan and any necessary medication, if applicable

All technology, including laptops, robots and other equipment, is provided. No need to bring a computer.

FOOD & HEALTH

Please do not bring nuts or fresh bananas. We have an allergy to fresh bananas; foods containing banana are okay.

Let us know about food allergies, medical needs, medication or anything that will help us support your child safely.

Please keep children home if they have cold, flu or other contagious symptoms, even if mild. Tell us if your child won't be attending.

FIND THE LAB

Level 1, 230 Marrickville Road, Marrickville NSW 2204. We're upstairs beside Pizza Hut, near the Victoria Road intersection.

Time-limited street parking may be available on Fernbank, Gladstone, Calvert and Victoria Roads, or try the council car park on Garners Avenue. Check parking signs.

Get directions to the Lab.

BEFORE DAY ONE

Please read the Booking Terms together, including expectations around safe and respectful behaviour, equipment and online activities.

We welcome beginners and experienced makers. If there are support needs, triggers or strategies that help your child enjoy the day, please tell us before they arrive.

Questions or changes? Text 0405 950 850. Our Child Safety policy is also available to read.
