YOUR PRIVACY

What information do we collect, why do we need it and how do we look after it?

This policy explains how we handle the information families and other visitors share with us. Qwerty Labs, formerly QwertyKids ("Qwerty"), handles information in accordance with the privacy laws that apply to our activities.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with Qwerty, we may collect:

  • Names, contact details, enquiries and correspondence.
  • Children's names, ages, bookings, attendance and parent or carer details.
  • Emergency contacts, authorised collection arrangements and information relevant to safe participation, including allergies, medical needs, accessibility and learning support requirements.
  • Payment and transaction records, and information provided through booking or payment services.
  • Feedback, student work and photographs or videos where collected for a stated purpose.
  • Technical information from our website and embedded services, such as device details, IP addresses, cookies and usage information.

We generally collect information directly from you, from a parent or carer, or through services used to make enquiries and bookings. We ask for information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant activity and try not to collect more than we need.

Why we use information

We use information to answer enquiries; manage bookings and payments; teach and support participants; manage attendance, collection and emergencies; communicate service information; maintain safety; improve our activities; and meet legal obligations.

We may send occasional news about our programs and projects where permitted by law. Marketing messages identify Qwerty and provide a way to unsubscribe. Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop essential booking or safety communications.

Children and sensitive information

We recognise that information about children, health, disability and support needs calls for additional care. We use it for safety, accessibility and appropriate learning support, restrict access to people who reasonably need it, and do not use it for unrelated publicity or marketing.

Parents and carers can contact us about their child's information. We also take account of a young person's age, understanding, wishes and privacy interests when responding to requests.

Photos, videos and student work

We ask for appropriate permission before publishing identifiable images or videos of children or connecting their names to publicly shared projects. Participation in a program is not automatic permission for promotion. We consider the child's own wishes as well as parent or carer permission.

You may request that Qwerty stop future use and remove material from channels we control. We will consider and act on such requests appropriately, although we may not be able to retrieve copies already shared by others.

Qwerty does not claim ownership of a student's original work simply because it was made in our studio. Any use of third-party software, content or platforms remains subject to their applicable licences and terms.

Online learning, AI and external services

Our teaching may involve learning websites, coding platforms, cloud storage, collaboration tools and AI services. We take account of the age suitability, privacy settings and terms of services selected for students. When an activity requires a child's own account or disclosure of personal information to an external service, we seek appropriate authorisation and provide relevant information.

We do not intentionally place children's private health information, identifying details or identifiable photographs into public projects or AI tools without an appropriate purpose and safeguards.

Booking, payments, email, website hosting, video embeds and other services may be provided by third parties. These services may collect or process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Some service providers may store or process information overseas, including in countries where their systems or subcontractors operate. You can contact us for information about services used for a particular activity.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We share information only where reasonably necessary for our services, where you authorise it, or where required or permitted by law. This may include payment and booking providers, technology services, emergency responders and child protection authorities.

We take reasonable steps to select and use service providers appropriately and to limit access to information to what is needed.

Keeping information safe

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to reduce the risk of loss, unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. We restrict access to sensitive records, review what we retain, and securely delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed and no legal retention requirement applies.

If an information security incident occurs, we assess the potential harm, take steps to contain it and notify affected people and regulators where required by law or otherwise appropriate.

YOUR RIGHTS

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request access or correction, or raise a privacy concern. Contact us using the details below. We may ask for evidence of identity or authority before releasing information, especially information about a child. Access may be subject to lawful exceptions and the rights of other people.

We will consider complaints and explain the outcome. Where an applicable privacy law gives you external review or complaint rights, you may be able to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or the Information and Privacy Commission NSW.

Changes

We update this policy when our services, information practices or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on our website.
