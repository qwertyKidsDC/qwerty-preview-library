PWYWpay what you want

ROOM TO MAKE

If fees make joining difficult, please talk to us.

When places are available, we can sometimes offer a weekly class or camp place for whatever you can afford, even nothing.

No awkward questions.

WHAT YOU PAY

Tell us what you can comfortably contribute. It might be the usual fee, a smaller amount, $5 or nothing at all.

There's no application form, no need to prove anything and no awkward questions. Once we've agreed on a place, your child gets the same teaching, tools and time as everyone else.

FINDING A PLACE

Flexible-fee places depend on the spaces we have available. For holiday camps, we may suggest particular days; for weekly classes, particular sessions. We'll work out the options with you before booking.

Not every session has space, but we'll do our best to find one that works.

NO STRINGS

If you'd like to contribute more later, or help another young maker have a turn, that's lovely. If not, that's completely fine. There's nothing to pay back.

LET'S TALK

If we've sent you this page, just reply to our message. Otherwise, get in touch or call 0405 950 850. Tell us which class or camp you're interested in, and we'll see what we can make work.
