identity
brief

working version / not final brand rules

The idea

Design Qwerty Labs as a place where people get hands-on with new technology together. It should feel precise and highly digital, but visibly inhabited by people who make, edit, test, break, repair and reshape things.

Start here

Do not start by trying to invent the logo. Build a webpage that creates the QL world. Let typography, surfaces, signals, edits, grids, glitches, colour, interaction and movement show us what the eventual mark needs to belong to.

Use history as material, not costume

Early internet / Y2K is useful because of the feeling underneath it: visible systems, imperfect connection, handmade corners and shared discovery. Translate those behaviours into a current AI-age identity. Do not make a 1999-themed website.

Push

  • precise digital structure + rough human traces
  • visible editing, revision and intervention
  • signals, joins, glitches and imperfect transfer with a reason
  • Atkinson Hyperlegible Next + Mono as the first type system
  • colour that is electric but warm enough to feel inhabited
  • interactions that show agency: select, drag, reveal, leave a trace

Avoid

  • generic glitch overlays
  • cyberpunk / AI-future chrome
  • retro-computer cosplay
  • crafty / cute “handmade” styling
  • coding-school, SaaS or generic creative-agency shorthand
  • neubrutalism used as a ready-made template

Logo architecture is open

Qwerty Labs, QwertyLabs, qwertyLabs, colour-separated words, a wordmark, a symbol, letters or no letters are all still live. The system gets to earn the answer.

Phase 04 / first graphic system

A first
visual answer.

The page you are looking at is itself the first output: one synthesis, not a final direction. It deliberately combines rough field + precise interface + visible system + shared signal.

Graphic behaviour A

Rough field / clean frame

Unfinished colour and texture can live behind crisp geometry. The roughness is substrate; the interface stays competent.

Graphic behaviour B

Presence signal

Dots, rings, relays and connection lines can represent participation without turning into generic “connected nodes”. They should behave like signs of activity, not a corporate network diagram.

Graphic behaviour C

Current + trace

Atkinson provides the disciplined contemporary layer. Marks, selection frames, noise and imperfect colour carry the human evidence.

Click anywhere on the page to leave a temporary local “signal”. It is intentionally tiny: the interaction is evidence that somebody was here, not a gimmick competing with the work.
